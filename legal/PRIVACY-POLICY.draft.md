# Privacy Policy

> **DRAFT: pending founder review and legal review. Not yet legally binding.**

**Effective date:** January 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Serendipity Initiatives, LLC ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use The Cancer Card (the "Service").

The Cancer Card exists to make it easier to ask for and offer help during cancer treatment. That only works if you can trust us with your information. This policy explains, in plain language, what we collect, why, who can see it, and what control you have over it.

## The short version

- We collect only what we need to run the Service: your name, email, an optional phone number, and the activity you choose to record (tasks, self-care logs, crew membership).
- **We never sell your information to third parties or advertisers. Full stop.**
- The people in your crew can see the tasks and activity you share with that crew. That visibility is the whole point of the app, and we spell it out below.
- You can ask us to access, correct, export, or delete your information at any time.
- The Cancer Card is a coordination tool, not a medical service, and we are not a healthcare provider.

The rest of this policy fills in the details.

## 1. Information we collect

We collect information you give us directly:

- **Account information:** your name and email address. If you choose to sign in by SMS, your phone number.
- **Crew information:** who is in your crew, who you have invited, and the role you hold (Card Holder or crew member).
- **Task activity:** the help requests you create (for example meals, rides, errands, or company), who claims and completes them, and related messages or notes you add.
- **Self-care logs:** entries you choose to record, such as rest, hydration, walks, and meals.
- **Activity history:** points, streaks, badges, and a record of actions taken in the app.

We also collect a small amount of technical information needed to keep the Service working and secure, such as session data and basic logs (for example, timestamps and error records).

**A note on sensitivity.** We do not collect medical records, diagnoses, or treatment details. But we are honest with ourselves about this: using The Cancer Card can reveal that you or someone you care about is going through cancer treatment. We treat all of your information as sensitive for that reason, and we hold it to a higher standard of care than a typical consumer app might.

## 2. How we use your information

We use your information to:

- Create and maintain your account and crew.
- Run the core features of the Service: posting tasks, claiming and completing them, tracking points and self-care, and showing activity to your crew.
- Send you notifications you have opted into, such as email or SMS about task updates and crew activity.
- Keep the Service secure, diagnose problems, and prevent abuse.
- Respond when you contact us for help.

We do not use your information for anything unrelated to running the Service.

## 3. We do not sell your information

**We never sell your personal information to third parties or advertisers.** We do not rent it, trade it, or share it for anyone else's marketing.

Looking ahead: at some point in the future, we may offer our users the option to see offers or messages from third parties inside the app. If that ever happens, it would work like this: the advertiser would not receive your identity, your contact information, or anything else about you unless you personally chose to engage with that advertiser. Nothing about you would be sold or exposed behind the scenes. This is not something we do today, and if we ever introduce it, we will update this policy first and make the change clear.

## 4. Who can see your information

**Your crew.** This is the heart of the app, so we want to be direct about it. When a Card Holder posts a task, records activity, or earns points, the members of their crew can see that within the crew. Crew members can see the Card Holder's task requests, who claimed and completed them, and related activity history. If you are a Card Holder, assume that anything you post in the app is visible to the people you have invited. If you are a crew member, your claims, completions, and activity within the crew are visible to the Card Holder and the rest of the crew.

Self-care logs belong to the Card Holder. Where the app shares self-care milestones with the crew (for example, as part of points and streaks), that sharing is part of the feature and visible in the app itself.

**Service providers.** We use a small number of providers to run the Service. Each one receives only what it needs to do its job:

- **Logto** (self-hosted identity provider): handles sign-in, including password, email magic-link, SMS, Google, and Apple sign-in. It processes your email, and your phone number if you use SMS sign-in.
- **Resend:** delivers transactional email, such as invitations and notifications. It processes your email address and the content of those messages.
- **Amazon Web Services (AWS):** stores encrypted off-site backups of our database.

We do not have "data partners", analytics resellers, or ad networks with access to your information.

**Legal requirements.** We may disclose information if we are legally required to, for example in response to a valid court order. If the law allows it, we will tell you before we do.

## 5. Legal basis for processing

Where laws such as the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) apply, we process your information on these bases:

- **Contract:** most processing is necessary to provide the Service you signed up for.
- **Consent:** optional features, such as SMS sign-in and non-essential notifications, rely on your consent, which you can withdraw.
- **Legitimate interests:** limited processing for security, abuse prevention, and keeping the Service reliable.

## 6. Data retention and account deletion

We keep your information for as long as your account is active.

**When a Card Holder deletes their account,** here is exactly what happens:

- We **anonymize** your personal information: your name, email address, phone number, and other contact details are scrubbed from our records.
- We **preserve the crew's shared history**: tasks, completions, points, and activity records remain, no longer connected to your identity. We do this so the people in your crew are not left with a hollowed-out record of the help they gave and received.
- We **notify your crew** that the Card Holder has left, so no one is left wondering.

Crew members can also request deletion of their accounts, with the same anonymization approach: personal identifiers are scrubbed, shared crew history is preserved.

Encrypted backups are retained on a rolling schedule and expire over time; anonymization carries through as backups cycle out.

If you want your account deleted, contact us at info@serendipitytechnology.com or use the in-app option where available.

## 7. Security

No one can promise perfect security, and we will not pretend otherwise. Here is what we actually do:

- **Encryption in transit:** all connections to the Service use HTTPS/TLS.
- **Access controls:** production data lives on a private server with restricted access, and crew data is only visible to members of that crew.
- **Encrypted backups:** off-site backups are encrypted before they leave our infrastructure.
- **Password protection:** passwords are hashed, never stored in plain text. Identity is handled through Logto rather than hand-rolled sign-in code.

If we ever discover a breach affecting your personal information, we will notify you as required by law, and we will aim to be faster and plainer about it than the law requires.

## 8. Your rights

You can ask us to:

- **Access** the personal information we hold about you.
- **Correct** information that is wrong or out of date.
- **Export** your information in a portable format.
- **Delete** your account, with the anonymization behavior described in Section 6.

To exercise any of these rights, email info@serendipitytechnology.com. We will respond within the timelines required by applicable law. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and we will honor those rights where they apply.

## 9. Children's privacy

The Cancer Card is not for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. If we learn that we have, we will delete it.

Users aged 13 to 17 may use the Service only with the consent of a parent or legal guardian. Cancer touches whole families, including teenagers who want to help, and we welcome them with a parent or guardian's permission.

## 10. Where your information is stored

Your information is stored on servers operated by us, with encrypted backups held by AWS. Our infrastructure is located in Florida, USA. If you use the Service from another country, you understand that your information will be processed where our servers are located, and we apply the protections in this policy regardless of where you are.

## 11. Cookies and session storage

We use only essential cookies and session storage: the minimum needed to keep you signed in and keep the Service secure. We do not use advertising cookies, cross-site tracking, or third-party analytics cookies.

## 12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date at the top and, for meaningful changes, notify you in the app or by email before the change takes effect. We will not quietly weaken your protections.
